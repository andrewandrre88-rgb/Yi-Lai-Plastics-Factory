Automatic production lines

Yi Lai Plastics Industry has made dispensing products for more than 15 years. Over 200 people work across 65+ injection moulding machines and 13+ automatic assembly lines, so moulding, assembly and packing happen in one place. That keeps lead times short and quality under our own control.

We work mainly with packaging importers, distributors and brands in cosmetics, personal care and household chemicals. Most of our shipments go to the Middle East and South America.

From resin to container

Four steps, all handled by our own team.

STEP 01

Moulding

Parts are injection moulded on our own machines from virgin PP and PE resin. RESIN GRADES TO CONFIRM

STEP 02

Assembly

Springs, pistons, gaskets and dip tubes are fitted on automatic assembly lines.

STEP 03

Quality check

Every batch is checked for output, leakage and fit on the bottle before packing. TEST DETAILS TO CONFIRM

STEP 04

Export shipping

Cartons are packed for sea or air freight from Ningbo port and shipped worldwide.
