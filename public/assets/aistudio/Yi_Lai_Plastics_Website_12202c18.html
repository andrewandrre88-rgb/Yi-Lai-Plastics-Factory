How an order runs

Four stages, all in the same plant.

  1. Mould

    Injection machines form the actuator, closure, housing and piston in your colour.

  2. Assemble

    Automatic lines fit springs, gaskets and dip tubes, cut to your bottle height.

  3. Check

    Pumps are pressed and checked for output and leaks before packing.

  4. Ship

    Cartons are packed for export and leave through Ningbo port.
